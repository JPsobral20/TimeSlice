| *Name* | TimeSlice |
| *Actor* | Student/Faculty Member | 
| *Description* | The student or faculty member is able to choose a pre-planned focus timer or personalise a new one. During the focus time set the user cannot leave the app and therefore can’t be distracted by their phone. If they are focus during a pre-determined amount of time this will result in coins that can be exchanged for goods at FEUP’s bar and it will also contribute to a scholarship funded by FEUP that goes to students in need.  |
| *Preconditions* | - The customer is a student or a faculty member at FEUP. |
| *Postconditions* | - The customer wins something from the faculty. <br> - The customer "contributes" for a scholarship. |
| *Normal flow* | 1. The customer accesses the appication. <br> 2. The system shows a timer. <br> 3. The customer selects the timer for a specific time. <br> 4. The customer does is job (studies, ...). <br> 5. The system get the counter running. <br> 6. The system gives the customer his reward. <br> 7. The customer goe sto the shop in the appication. <br> 8. The system shows every option available to the customer <br> 9. The customer buys one option.|
| *Alternative flows and exceptions* | 1. In step 2, the customer can choose a study plan given by the system. |
